4. Forkcast Publisher (TikTok and YouTube integration)

Forkcast Publisher is used only by the Forkcast team to upload Forkcast's own videos to Forkcast's own accounts. It has no public sign-up and does not post on behalf of anyone else.

TikTok

When our own TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, TikTok provides us with: the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar), an access token and refresh token, and creator information needed for posting (for example available privacy settings and maximum video length). We use this data only to upload and publish our own videos and to show the posting status. We do not request, read or store data about viewers, followers or other TikTok users. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube

Forkcast Publisher uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own YouTube channel. By using it we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and data processed through the YouTube API Services is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. We access only our own channel (channel ID and upload permission) and store only the OAuth tokens required to upload. Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in our private publishing environment and are never published. They are deleted when the connection is revoked or no longer needed. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or in the Google account settings.

5. Our social media profiles

When you visit or interact with Forkcast on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the respective platform processes your data under its own privacy policy. We only see the information the platforms show to every account owner, such as public comments and aggregated statistics.
