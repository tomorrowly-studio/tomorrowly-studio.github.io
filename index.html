Forkcast by Tomorrowly

Four futures, one fork at a time.

Forkcast is a short-form video series. Each season takes one big question about the future — food, cities, work, energy, health — and maps four distinct futures for it.

How it works

We pick two real, sourced drivers of change and combine them in a classic 2×2 scenario matrix. Every episode explores one of the four resulting worlds: what daily life could look like, what it would take to get there (research, regulation, the people already working on it), and which startup ideas that future would open up.

Scenarios are not forecasts. Every factual claim is sourced, every speculative element is labeled as a scenario, and AI-generated visuals and voices are disclosed on every platform.

Where to watch

Forkcast publishes on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. Channel links follow at launch.

Forkcast Publisher

Forkcast Publisher is our internal publishing tool. It uploads finished Forkcast episodes to Forkcast's own social media accounts after a human has approved each season. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account. Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
