Four futures.
One question.

Forkcast is a short video series about the future. Each season takes one big question and maps four ways it could turn out.

How an episode works

  1. The question

    One big question about food, cities, work, energy or health.

  2. Two drivers

    Two real, sourced forces that will shape the answer and could go either way.

  3. The matrix

    Crossing the two drivers gives four distinct futures.

  4. Inside one future

    What everyday life could look like, and what it would take to get there.

  5. Startup ideas

    The companies that future would need, from research to regulation.

What you can rely on

Every fact is sourced

Claims come with a source. Anything we cannot back up does not make it into an episode.

Scenarios, not forecasts

We never claim to know what will happen. We show what would have to be true for each future.

AI is labeled

Visuals and voices are partly AI-generated, and we say so on every platform.
